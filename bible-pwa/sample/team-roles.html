What each role can do

Admin

  • Everything an editor can do
  • Add, remove and change people on this page
  • Publish this church's Today's service to the Nau Mpro App
  • Rename the church and change the join code
  • See app visits and installs

Editor

  • Edit any song, reading and program, not only their own
  • Build Scripture slides and presentations for the team
  • Undo a change from the history

Anyone else

  • Read songs, readings and the Bible
  • Use the Projector and Present
  • Keep their own notes and drafts
  • Can't change the team's songs or readings

Shared by all churches

  • The Bunong Bible
  • Songs, readings, programs and slides belong to one church

Roles are per church: someone can be an admin here and an editor elsewhere. There must always be at least one admin. You can't remove yourself or lower your own role.
