When people are told

  • Someone enters your join code: every admin of that church gets a notification, and can accept or decline it right there.
  • Someone is added (request accepted, or invited by email and signed in): they’re told who added them and their role.
  • A role changes or someone is removed: that person is told.
  • A new church waits for acceptance: site admins are told.
  • The bell checks every minute and when you come back to the page. Email can come later.
